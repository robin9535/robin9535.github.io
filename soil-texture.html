How this works

  1. Texture uses the fine earth only. Gravel (larger than 2 mm) is left out. Sand, silt and clay are re-scaled so they add up to 100%.
  2. Size limits follow the USDA system: sand 2 to 0.05 mm, silt 0.05 to 0.002 mm, clay below 0.002 mm. A lab that uses a 0.075 mm sieve, as in engineering soil tests, will find slightly less sand than this system expects.
  3. Reading the chart: clay is read on the left edge, silt on the right edge and sand on the bottom edge. The dashed guide lines run from your point to each axis, so you can see where each value sits.
  4. The class is calculated from the standard USDA boundaries. Points close to a boundary are flagged, because a small lab error can change the class.
  5. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere.
