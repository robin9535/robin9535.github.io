EIA field visit kit

Walk the site with a guided baseline checklist, then score the likely impacts of each project activity in a simple matrix. You get ranked impacts, mitigation notes and a printable summary to start your EIA report from.

Project and site

Baseline observations

Answer what you can see and hear on the visit. Choose a condition for each component group and write what you noticed. "Not observed" is a valid answer; do not guess.

Impact matrix

Tap a cell to score the effect of an activity on a component. Give a direction (harmful or beneficial), a magnitude from 1 to 5 and an importance from 1 to 5. The cell value is magnitude × importance, negative for harmful effects.

High adverseModerate adverseLow adverseBeneficial

Convention used on this page (adjust it to your Terms of Reference): absolute value 1–6 is low, 8–12 is moderate, 15–25 is high.

Mitigation notes

Output

Your work is stored in this browser only. Save the project file to keep a copy or to move it to another device. This kit helps you organise field observations and a screening-level matrix; it does not replace the scoping, baseline measurements, public consultation and expert judgement that a full EIA requires.
