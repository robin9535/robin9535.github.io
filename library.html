Adding your files

  1. Open the folder above and go into your course’s subfolder.
  2. Press New then File upload, or drag your file in.
  3. Name it clearly, for example Course code – Assignment 2 – Your name.
  4. Use PDF where you can, so it opens on any phone.
Please keep it fair. Share your own work as a reference, and follow your teachers’ rules on copying. The folder is private to people the owner adds, so the link alone will not open it.
