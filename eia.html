Environmental impact assessment, stage by stage

Tools for each step of an EIA study: scope the site, measure the baseline in the field, then predict what the project will do to air, water and noise. Built for environmental science students and field teams in Bangladesh.

Scope the siteMeasure the baselinePredict the impactBackground science
1

Scope the site

Before the field visit: find the boundary, see what surrounds the site, and decide what the study has to cover.

EIA Field Visit KitBaseline checklist and an impact matrix you fill in on site. GIS Area ExplorerBoundary, elevation and modelled air pollution for any area in Bangladesh. Earth Engine AnalyzerSatellite analysis of land cover and change around the site. WebGIS LabElevation profiles and ready-made Earth Engine scripts.
2

Measure the baseline

On site: record what the environment is like now, so there is something to compare the prediction against.

Sound Level MeterMeasure noise in decibels with a phone microphone. Water Quality IndexScore a water sample against WHO and Bangladesh limits. Soil Texture TrianglePlot sand, silt and clay and read the soil class. Quadrat CounterTally species and get diversity indices. Field NotebookSlope, height and distance measurements using only a phone. Field TrackerRecord a GPS track and waypoints as you walk the site. EcoLens WeatherLive weather and the chance of rain for the survey day.
3

Predict the impact

Back at the desk: change the project and see how far the effect reaches and whether a limit is broken.

Noise with DistanceSite noise at a receptor, with background, against day and night limits.New Air DispersionGaussian plume from a stack and ground-level concentration downwind. River Oxygen SagStreeter-Phelps: where a discharge pulls dissolved oxygen lowest. Pumping Well and GroundwaterTheis drawdown around a tubewell and interference between two wells.

Background science

The systems an assessment sits inside. Useful for coursework and for explaining a prediction to someone else.

Atmosphere SimulatorThree-cell circulation, Coriolis deflection and the monsoon shift. Greenhouse Energy BalanceAlbedo, greenhouse strength and surface temperature. Carbon Cycle Box ModelFollow emitted carbon through air, land and ocean. Rock Cycle SimulatorWeathering to metamorphism with a thin-section view.
