Try this

  • Press 400 km, the height of a low Earth orbit. Compare the speed and the time for one orbit with the geostationary preset.
  • At 35 786 km the period is about 24 hours. That is why the satellite seems to stay above one spot.
  • Choose launch speed. Below the circular speed the path becomes an ellipse. What happens if you go too low?
  • Go above the circular speed. Find the speed where the satellite never comes back.

The equations

v = √(GM / r)    r = R + h
T = 2π √(r³ / GM)
vescape = √(2GM / r) = √2 · v
gh = GM / r²

Uses GM = 3.986 × 1014 m³/s² and R = 6371 km for the Earth. Your textbook may round these, so small differences are normal. The animation is sped up so one circular orbit takes about 10 seconds.
