Try this

  • With no friction, release the ball and watch the bars. Potential and kinetic swap, but their sum stays the same.
  • Where is the ball fastest? Check that it matches v = √(2gh) for the height it fell.
  • Double the mass. Does the speed at the bottom change?
  • On the double well, start high on one side. Can the ball cross the hump in the middle, and reach the same height on the far side?
  • Turn friction on. Where does the missing mechanical energy go?

The equations

PE = mgh    KE = ½ m v²
PE + KE = constant (no friction)
v = √(2g Δh)
With friction: Elost = Estart − (PE + KE)

g = 9.8 m/s². Height is measured from the bottom of the bowl, the lowest point of the track. The lost energy becomes heat.
