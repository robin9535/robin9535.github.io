Physical optics

Live experiments with the light source, slits and screen drawn in front of you: Young's double slit, single-slit diffraction, the diffraction grating, Malus's law and Brewster's angle. The waves move in real time. Change a setting and the waves, the pattern and the formulas all update together.

What to look for

Results come from the standard textbook formulas. The bright and dark pattern on the screen is drawn on a gamma scale so that dim fringes stay visible; compare heights on the graph for true relative intensity. This page is a study aid; check formulas and constants against your textbook.
