Simple Pendulum

Do the pendulum practical on screen. Time ten swings, work out g from the period, then find the gravity of a planet nobody has told you about.

Set up the pendulum

Press Release, then Time 10 swings. The stopwatch starts the next time the bob passes the middle.

Try this

  • Time 10 swings at 1.00 m, then at 0.25 m. The length is a quarter. What happens to the period?
  • Keep the length fixed and compare a release of 5° with 70°. The formula says the period should not change. Does it?
  • Choose the unknown planet, time 10 swings, and work out g yourself before you press the button to check.
  • Mass is not a control here. Look at the formula and say why.

The equations

T = 2π √(L / g)
g = 4π² L / T²

The formula is exact only for small swings. This model solves the full equation of motion, so at large angles the measured period comes out longer than the formula, exactly as a real pendulum does. That is why the practical asks for a small release angle. Air resistance and the mass of the string are ignored.
