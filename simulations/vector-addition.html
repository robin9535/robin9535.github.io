Vector Addition

Change the size and direction of two vectors, or drag their tips, and watch the resultant follow the parallelogram law.

Resultant |R|
Direction of R from A
Dot product A·B
Cross product |A×B|

Set the vectors

You can also drag the tip of A or B on the picture. Angles are measured from the +x axis.

Try this

  • Set both vectors to the same direction. The resultant is the plain sum A + B.
  • Turn B to the opposite direction. The resultant is the difference of the sizes.
  • Press “Make A ⊥ B”. Check that |R| = √(A² + B²) and that the dot product is 0.
  • Switch to A − B. The second arrow flips, and the resultant is the other diagonal of the parallelogram.

The equations

R = √(A² + B² + 2AB cosθ)
tan α = B sinθ / (A + B cosθ)
A·B = AB cosθ    |A×B| = AB sinθ
Components: Ax = A cosφ, Ay = A sinφ

θ is the angle between the two vectors, and α is the angle R makes with A. For A − B the same formulas hold with B reversed.
