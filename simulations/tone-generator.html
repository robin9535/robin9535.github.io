Tone generator

Play a pure tone at any frequency from 20 Hz to 20,000 Hz. Switch the wave shape, sweep through the range, mix two tones to hear beats, and see the sound's wavelength, musical note and waveform. A companion to the Sound Level Meter.

Protect your hearing. Start with the volume low and raise it slowly. Loud tones, especially high ones, can hurt your ears and damage speakers. Do not use headphones at a high level.

What you are hearing

Waveform
Spectrum (frequency content)

Sound travels through air at about 331.3 + 0.606 × T metres per second (T in °C), so the wavelength is λ = speed ÷ frequency. Notes use equal temperament with A4 = 440 Hz. Most young people hear roughly 20 Hz to 20,000 Hz, and the upper limit falls with age and noise exposure. Many phone and laptop speakers cannot produce low frequencies below about 100 Hz, so you may see a tone you cannot hear; that is the speaker, not the page. This page makes sound through your device and does not measure it. To measure real noise, use the Sound Level Meter.
