Calibrate for better accuracy

Every phone microphone is different, and some phones process the sound before the browser sees it. Without calibration the number can be 10 dB or more off.

  1. Place your phone beside a reference sound level meter (or a calibrator) in a steady noise, such as a fan or a speaker playing noise.
  2. Start listening here, wait a few seconds, then type the reference meter's reading below.
  3. Press Calibrate. The offset is saved on this phone.
