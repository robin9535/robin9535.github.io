Written 12 November 2026 from a scan of the repository. The licence names below come from general knowledge, not from reading each licence file, so **verify each one against the official source before relying on it in a sale or licensing agreement.** This is not legal advice.

| Item | Where | Licence (to verify) | Note for a buyer |

| Bricolage Grotesque, Source Serif 4 (Google Fonts) | Every page, loaded from fonts.googleapis.com | SIL Open Font License 1.1 | Free to use and bundle. For offline use, download the font files and self-host them. |

## Textbook constants

Values such as g = 9.8 m/s², GM and R for the Earth, Young's modulus and yield stresses, and the speed of sound are typical textbook values entered from memory. They have not been checked against the NCTB books. A buyer will expect them to be checked.
