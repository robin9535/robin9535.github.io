How to learn from a lab

  1. Predict first

    Before you touch a slider, say what you think will happen. Each lab lists questions to start from.

  2. Change one thing

    Move a single control and leave the rest alone, the way you would in a real experiment.

  3. Check with the equation

    Every lab shows the formula it uses. Put your numbers in and see if your answer matches the screen.
