Studying how water, soil and people shape each other

I am pursuing a B.Sc. in Environmental Science and Engineering at Jatiya Kabi Kazi Nazrul Islam University in Trishal, Mymensingh, in the 9th batch (session 2023–24). My coursework runs from soil mechanics, limnology and oceanography to biological wastewater treatment and GIS mapping.

Outside class I work in youth leadership, scouting and volunteering. I like the point where environmental science meets public policy and community awareness, because that is where findings turn into action.

Field study, 2026

Environmental impact assessment of the Someshwari River

A field-based assessment in Durgapur and Bijoypur, Netrokona. I took on-site samples and analysed soil, water quality and the acoustic environment of the surrounding ecosystem.

Blue economy and coastal management in Bangladesh
