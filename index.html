About Me

I am pursuing my B.Sc. in Environmental Science and Engineering at Jatiya Kabi Kazi Nazrul Islam University (JKKNIU), Trishal, Mymensingh. My academic journey focuses on understanding complex environmental systems—ranging from soil mechanics, limnology, and oceanography to biological wastewater treatment and GIS mapping.

Beyond academics, I actively engage in youth leadership, scouting, and social impact initiatives, working to bridge the gap between environmental science, public policy, and community awareness.
