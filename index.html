Tools for academic study and research

Live weather, a phone sound meter, an atmosphere simulator and a shared study library, free for university students and anyone doing research. Made by a JKKNIU student.

Tools and resources

EcoLens Weather

Add a photo and a place to see live weather, the chance of rain over the next 24 hours, and the rain and cloud drawn over your picture. Your photo never leaves your device.

Open EcoLens

Sound Level Meter

Measure noise in decibels with your phone's microphone. Built for quick field checks, with calibration and 10-second measurements.

Open the meter

Atmosphere Simulator

Interactive model of the Hadley, Ferrel and Polar cells, with Coriolis deflection and the seasonal shift of the ITCZ that drives the monsoon.

Launch simulator

Soil Texture Triangle

Enter sieve and hydrometer results, see the soil plotted on the USDA texture triangle with guide lines to each axis, and get the soil class.

Open the triangle

Rock Cycle & Petrology Simulator

Explore weathering, sedimentation, lithification and metamorphism with a live thin-section microscope and crustal P–T–W controls.

Launch rock cycle

WebGIS, Topography & Pollution Lab

Extract area boundaries, analyze DEM elevation profiles, map real-time air pollution rates, and generate Python (geemap) & GEE scripts.

Open WebGIS lab

GIS Area Explorer

Search any area in Bangladesh, get its boundary, elevation and modelled air pollution, draw and measure, and export GeoJSON.

Open explorer

Field Notebook

Measure tree or building height and strike and dip with just your phone, then analyse and export your data.

Open notebook

Water Quality Calculator

Compare water samples with WHO or Bangladesh limits and get a Water Quality Index.

Open calculator

Field Tracker

Record a GPS track and waypoints with notes while you survey, see distance and area, and export GPX, GeoJSON or CSV.

Open tracker

Earth Engine Analyzer

Pick or draw any area and get satellite vegetation, heat, NO2, rainfall, land cover, water and elevation from your own Earth Engine account.

Open analyzer

Quadrat Counter

Tally species quadrat by quadrat with big tap buttons, then get density, frequency, importance value and Shannon and Simpson diversity.

Open counter

Air Dispersion Model

Gaussian plume model for a stack or chimney: peak ground-level concentration, where it lands, a receptor value and a plan-view plume map.

Open model

EIA Field Visit Kit

Guided baseline checklist for the site visit, a scored impact matrix, ranked adverse impacts, mitigation notes and a printable summary.

Open the kit

How to read the results

  • Weather comes from forecast models through Open-Meteo. It is a forecast for a place, not a measurement at your exact spot.
  • The Sound Level Meter uses a phone microphone. It is good for comparing and rough checks, not a replacement for a certified meter.
  • The Atmosphere Simulator is a simplified teaching model.
