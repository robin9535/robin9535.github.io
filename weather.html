        How this works

        1. Your photo is shown and drawn on in your browser. It is never uploaded. If the photo has a location tag or a time, they are read on your device, and the location is only used if you press the button to use it.
        2. The weather comes from Open-Meteo, which blends several national weather models. Air quality comes from the CAMS atmospheric model through the same service. Both are forecasts, not measurements at your exact spot.
        3. The rain chance is the model's chance of measurable rain in that hour. The rain you see on the picture reflects the expected amount of rain, so a 40% chance with almost no expected rain shows little or none.
        4. The picture effects are drawn from the weather numbers (rain amount, cloud cover, wind, day or night). The tool does not analyse what is in your photo.
        5. Saved pictures contain the photo, the effects and the card text only. Saving removes any hidden location data from the original file.
