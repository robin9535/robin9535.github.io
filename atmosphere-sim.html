Impact on Bangladesh & South Asia

With the sun directly over the Equator (0°), the ITCZ sits near the equatorial Indian Ocean. Bangladesh experiences warm transition weather between winter trades and summer monsoons.

0° – 30° Latitude

Hadley Cell (Thermally Direct)

ITCZ: ~1008 hPa

Driven directly by intense solar heating near the Equator. Warm, moist air rises at the Intertropical Convergence Zone (ITCZ), releases heavy convective rainfall, travels poleward at 12–15 km altitude, and sinks around 30° N/S at the Subtropical Highs (Horse Latitudes).
