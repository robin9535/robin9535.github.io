SSC physicsChapter 6 of 14: Effect of heat on matter

Heating ice until it boils

Start with ice at −20 °C and keep a heater on. Follow the temperature as the ice warms, melts, heats up as water and boils away.

Controls

Key ideas in this chapter

Heating without change of state: Q = mcΔθ
Melting or boiling: Q = ml
Temperature stays constant while the state is changing

Values used: c of ice 2100, water 4200 and steam 2000 J/kg °C; latent heat of fusion 336,000 J/kg and of vaporisation 2,268,000 J/kg. No heat is lost to the room.
