SSC physicsChapter 11 of 14: Current electricity

Series and parallel circuits

Connect a resistor and a bulb to a battery in series or in parallel. The dots show the current and the glow shows the power in the bulb.

Controls

Key ideas in this chapter

V = IR
Series: R = R₁ + R₂
Parallel: 1/R = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂
P = VI

Dots move in the direction of conventional current. The bulb is treated as a fixed resistance and the battery as having none.
