SSC physicsChapter 2 of 14: Motion

A car on a straight road

Give a car a starting velocity and an acceleration, then move time forward. The road shows where it is and the graph shows how its velocity changes.

Controls

Key ideas in this chapter

v = u + at
s = ut + ½at²
v² = u² + 2as
Slope of a velocity-time graph = acceleration; area under it = displacement

The acceleration is uniform, as in the equations of motion.
