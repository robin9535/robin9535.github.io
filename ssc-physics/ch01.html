SSC physicsChapter 1 of 14: Physical quantities and measurement

Reading slide calipers

Measure an object to a hundredth of a centimetre. Slide the jaw, read the main scale and the vernier scale, and let the teacher check your reading.

Controls

Key ideas in this chapter

Vernier constant = 1 main scale division − 1 vernier division = 0.01 cm
Length = main scale reading + vernier number × vernier constant

The scales are drawn enlarged. Real calipers also need a check for zero error before you measure.
