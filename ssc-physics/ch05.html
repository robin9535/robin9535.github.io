SSC physicsChapter 5 of 14: State of matter and pressure

Pressure under a liquid

Send a diver down into water, sea water, kerosene or mercury and see how the pressure builds with depth.

Controls

Key ideas in this chapter

Pressure in a liquid: P = hρg
Density: ρ = m / V
Pressure acts equally in all directions at the same depth

Atmospheric pressure is taken as 101.3 kPa and g as 9.8 m/s².
