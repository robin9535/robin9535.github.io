SSC physicsChapter 3 of 14: Force

Pushing a box

Push a box across a floor. Change the push, the mass and the friction, and see when it moves and how quickly it speeds up.

Controls

Key ideas in this chapter

Net force = push − friction
F = ma
Greatest friction = coefficient × weight

One friction coefficient is used for both rest and motion to keep the model simple. g = 9.8 m/s².
