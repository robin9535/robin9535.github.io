SSC physicsChapter 8 of 14: Reflection of light

Curved mirrors

Move an object in front of a concave or convex mirror. Two rays are traced for you and the image is found where they meet.

Controls

Key ideas in this chapter

Mirror equation: 1/u + 1/v = 1/f
Magnification: m = v / u
Radius of curvature: r = 2f

Sign rule: real is positive, so a virtual image has a negative v and a convex mirror a negative f. Heights are drawn three times larger than distances. For lenses, open the Lens Ray Diagram lab.
