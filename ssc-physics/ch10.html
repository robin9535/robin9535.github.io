SSC physicsChapter 10 of 14: Static electricity

Force between two charges

Place two charges, choose their sign and size, and move them apart. See which way the force points and how fast it falls with distance.

Controls

Key ideas in this chapter

F = k q₁q₂ / r², with k = 9 × 10⁹ N m²/C²
Like charges repel; unlike charges attract
Doubling the distance quarters the force

The charges are treated as points in air. Arrow lengths are compressed so that very large and very small forces both fit.
