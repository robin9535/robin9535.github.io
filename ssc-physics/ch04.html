SSC physicsChapter 4 of 14: Work, power and energy

A falling ball

Lift a ball to the top of a tower and let it fall. Watch potential energy turn into kinetic energy while the total stays the same.

Controls

Key ideas in this chapter

Potential energy = mgh
Kinetic energy = ½mv²
Total energy stays constant: mgh = ½mv² at the ground
Power = work ÷ time

Air resistance is ignored. g = 9.8 m/s².
