How the index is calculated

Weighted arithmetic WQI (Horton/Brown method). For each parameter measured: sub-index q = 100 × (V − Videal) / (S − Videal), where V is your reading, S the limit, and Videal is 0 (pH 7, dissolved oxygen 14.6 mg/L). Weight w = 1/S. WQI = Σ(q×w) / Σw, over the parameters you entered. For pH the deviation from 7 is used.

Classes: below 50 excellent, 50–100 good, 100–200 poor, 200–300 very poor, above 300 unsuitable for drinking. A WQI under 100 can still hide one parameter above its limit, which is why the table checks each one.
